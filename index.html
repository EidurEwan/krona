Krona

Krona is a personal finance tracker that one person runs on their own computer and phone, for their own bank accounts. It is non-commercial and not offered to anyone else.

Privacy

Krona reads account details, balances and transactions from accounts its owner has connected through Enable Banking. That data is stored only on the owner's own devices. It is not sent to this website or shared with anyone else. Krona cannot make payments.

Access can be ended at any time by withdrawing consent with the bank, and the data can be erased by deleting the local database.

Terms

Krona is for its owner's private use only. No service is provided to the public, and nothing here is financial advice.
